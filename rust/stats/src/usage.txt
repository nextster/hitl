Usage:
  hitl stats [--days N] [--comments] [--json]

  What the reviewer did not understand in the checks that ended, on the Mac and on the phone: every
  «не понял» with the item it was said to, the check, the chat and the agent, newest first, and
  the counts over them. Read it before changing how chats ask him: a rule for every chat goes to
  him first, as a plan he answers «да» or «нет».

  --days N    The last N days; 7 when not given, 0 for everything that is kept.
              Under the counts: what each chat took of him, its checks, items and minutes, the
              longest first. He wanted that count in this report only, not in the window.
  --comments  His other comments as well, under the same heads.
  --json      The same as JSON, an item a line, for a chat to read.

An item counts as not understood when he answered «не понял» (the key 0) or «не понятно» (to an
explanation), or wrote it in his own words in the comment. The text of an item is kept since
2026-10-11; of an older check only his comment is.

Under the counts the report lists the words he asked about in a comment («что за X», «что такое X»),
each with how many times: a word goes into the refusals of `hitl check` only on his «да».
--json adds to an item what it offered, where the history kept it (since 2026-11-03): options,
many, details, facts and the number of links, copies and images.
